03 / Boundaries

Browser-safe is not Node-compatible

No filesystem paths, Node streams, continuation state files, EventEmitter hooks, or OpenSSL encryption/decryption are available. Inputs are bytes, Blob, or File; outputs are Uint8Array.

Every reader, writer, modifier, copying context, and Recipe is ended or disposed. Registered assets are unregistered, and replacing a preview revokes its previous object URL.
